# A Document in Concert

*Semantic reading and the responsive document · Volume IV · Concept 013*

For a reader who knows what a semantic annotation is and wants to see what it makes possible while reading. Follow block 10 up the page during Photography focus, then back down to the same place when the focus opens.

The input is an illustrative, ten-passage sketch of Lewis Carroll. The script adapts the supplied narration to name visible examples and distinguish the two counting units. Cue times below are absolute seconds in the 30 fps master.
