# Mutable Motion

Work from the episode brief and its storyboard. Install and run animation tooling from this directory. Register each episode in the Studio composition list in index.tsx and share the workspace lockfile.

Author motion against absolute frame time so jumping to a cue gives the same picture as playing through it. Load the series fonts before measuring text. Keep the literary sentence in one text run and attach ranges to its measured glyph boundaries throughout each transformation.

Keep generated media in renders/. Inspect a completed encode at phone size, including the transitions between poses. Record intentional changes to timing, examples or branding in the episode storyboard.

## Editorial direction for Concepts

Concept films teach why an idea matters and how it works. Use a worked textual example, introduce terminology through visible changes, and make the episode intelligible as a standalone resource with the sound off. A product demonstration explains how to perform a task in a particular application and belongs in a separate series.

Keep narration, captions, diagrams and title cards independent of products. Omit product names, screenshots, promotional claims and comparisons. Use the abstract series mark, established typography, colours and episode number. Set `productBranding={false}` on the series plate for new concept films. Discreet `mutable.ink` attribution is optional. Keep completed earlier editions intact unless their revision is requested.
