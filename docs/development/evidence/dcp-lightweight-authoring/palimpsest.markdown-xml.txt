A <e ref="e1">**palimpsest**</e> is a manuscript page whose original writing has been scraped or washed away so it can be written on again, often leaving traces of the earlier text. Figuratively, a <e ref="e1">palimpsest</e> is something whose earlier forms or history remain visible beneath its present appearance.
